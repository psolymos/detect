# Bird point count data from Alberta's Oil Sands region

Data set used in Mahon et al. 2016 and Mahon et al. 2019.

## Usage

``` r
data("josm")
```

## Format

A list with elements `surveys`, `species`, and `counts`.

## Source

Mahon et al. 2016 and Mahon et al. 2019.

## References

Mahon, C. L., Holloway, G., Solymos, P., Cumming, S. G., Bayne, E. M.,
Schmiegelow, F. K. A., Song, S. J., 2016. Community structure and niche
characteristics of upland and lowland western boreal birds at multiple
spatial scales *Forest Ecology and Management* **361**:99-116.
\<doi:10.1016/j.foreco.2015.11.007\>

Mahon, C. L., Holloway, G., Bayne, E. M., Toms, J. D., 2019. Additive
and interactive cumulative effects on boreal landbirds: winners and
losers in a multi-stressor landscape *Ecological Applications*
**29**:e01895. \<doi:10.1002/eap.1895\>
